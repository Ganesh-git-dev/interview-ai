# DFIR Analyst (Senior)

**Company:** BlackPerl DFIR  
**Location:** Remote  
**Type:** Full-Time  
**Experience:** 5+ years

## Role Overview

We are seeking a Senior DFIR Analyst to lead digital forensic investigations and incident response engagements. You will handle complex cyber incidents, perform advanced forensic analysis, and guide our response strategy.

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead incident response engagements for enterprise clients
- Perform memory forensics, disk forensics, and network forensics
- Conduct malware analysis and reverse engineering
- Develop and maintain IR playbooks and runbooks
- Produce detailed forensic reports with findings and recommendations
- Mentor junior DFIR analysts
- Collaborate with threat intelligence teams

## Required Skills

- Deep expertise in memory forensics (Volatility, Rekall)
- Experience with disk forensics (FTK, EnCase, Autopsy)
- Strong knowledge of network forensics and PCAP analysis (Wireshark)
- Malware analysis and reverse engineering (IDA Pro, Ghidra, x64dbg)
- Experience with EDR platforms (CrowdStrike, SentinelOne)
- Log analysis across Windows, Linux, and cloud environments
- Scripting and automation (Python, PowerShell)

## Preferred Qualifications

- GCFA, GCFE, GREM, or equivalent SANS certifications
- Experience with cloud forensics (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- Published forensic methodology or tooling
- Law enforcement or legal proceeding testimony experience
- Experience with threat hunting and proactive detection

## Education

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Digital Forensics, or related field
- Advanced degree preferred
