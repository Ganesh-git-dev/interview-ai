# SOC Analyst (Entry-Level)

**Company:** CyberShield Solutions  
**Location:** Remote  
**Type:** Full-Time  
**Experience:** 0-2 years

## Role Overview

We are seeking a motivated SOC Analyst to join our Security Operations Center team. You will be responsible for monitoring security events, triaging alerts, and supporting incident response efforts. This is an excellent opportunity for early-career cybersecurity professionals.

## Key Responsibilities

- Monitor SIEM platforms (Splunk, ELK) for security events and alerts
- Triage and categorize incoming security incidents
- Escalate critical incidents to senior analysts
- Maintain documentation of security procedures and incident reports
- Support threat hunting activities
- Participate in shift-based rotation for 24/7 coverage

## Required Skills

- Basic understanding of network protocols (TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP)
- Familiarity with SIEM concepts and log analysis
- Knowledge of common attack vectors and threat actors
- Understanding of the cyber kill chain and MITRE ATT&CK framework
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent written and verbal communication

## Preferred Qualifications

- Security+ or equivalent certification
- Experience with Splunk or ELK stack
- Basic scripting skills (Python, PowerShell)
- Understanding of cloud security concepts (AWS, Azure)

## Education

- Bachelor's degree in Cybersecurity, Computer Science, or related field (or equivalent experience)
